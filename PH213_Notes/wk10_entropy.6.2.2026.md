#### Isothermal Work 
$$W_{\text{isothermal}} = nRT\ln\left(\frac{V_f}{V_i}\right)$$
#### Entropy 
Definition of entropy change
$$dS = \int_i^f\frac{\Delta Q_{reversible}}{T}\ dT$$
Entropy change for an ideal gas
$$\Delta S = nC_V\ln\left(\frac{T_f}{T_i}\right) + nR\ln\left(\frac{V_f}{V_i}\right)$$
Entropy is a **state function** it is not path dependant.

##### Example
2 identical blocks of 1.5kg copper. Each in insulated compartment.

$T_{iL}$ is 60C, $T_{iR}$ is 20C.

After shutter is lifted they come into thermal equilibrium at 40C. 

What is the net entropy of the 2 block system?

Specific heat of copper is $386\frac{J}{kg\ K}$
$$\Delta S_L = \int_i^f\frac{\Delta Q}{T} = \int_i^fmc\frac{dT}{T} = mc\times \ln\frac{T_F}{T_{iL}}$$
$$\Delta S_L = 1.5kg\times \frac{386J}{kg\ K}\times \ln\frac{331K}{333K} = -35.86\frac{J}{K}$$
$$\Delta S_R = 1.5kg\times \frac{386J}{kg\ K}\times \ln\frac{313K}{293K} = 38.23\frac{J}{K}$$
$$\Delta S_T = \Delta S_L + \Delta S_R = 38.23 - 35.86 = 2.37\frac{J}{K}$$
