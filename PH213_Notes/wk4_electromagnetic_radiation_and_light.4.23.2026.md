## Class 8
#### What is Light?
Light is an electromagnetic wave

It's wave speed is:
$$c = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}\approx 2.998\times 10^{8}$$

##### Poynting Vector
The direction of $\vec{S}$ gives the waves direction of travel and direction of energy transport at that point.
$$\vec{S} = \frac{1}{\mu_0}\vec{E}\times \vec{B} = \frac{1}{\mu_0}EB\sin(\theta)$$
##### EM Wave Speed
$$c = f\lambda$$
##### Index of Refraction
$$n = \frac{c}{v}$$
##### Reflection
$$\theta_1 = \theta_2$$
##### Refraction
$$n_1\sin\theta_1 = n_2\sin\theta_2$$
