## Class 15
#### Heat Capacity and Specific Heat
$$\text{heat} \equiv Q$$
$$Q = \Delta E_{internal} = C\Delta T = mc\Delta T$$
The symbol for heat is $Q$.

The heat absorbed by a substance is it's change in internal energy.

Heat is the heat capacity $\times$ the change in temperature **or** the specific heat $\times$ the mass $\times$ the change in temperature.

5kJ will raise the temperature of 100g of
###### Water
$$\Delta T = \frac{5kJ}{0.1kg \times 4.18\frac{kJ}{kg K}} = 12.0K$$
###### Copper
$$\Delta T = \frac{5kJ}{0.1kg \times 0.386\frac{kJ}{kg K}} = 129.5.0K$$
###### Mercury
$$\Delta T = \frac{5kJ}{0.1kg \times 0.14\frac{kJ}{kg K}} = 357K$$
**Specific Heat**: Heat capacity of a substance per mass or per mole ($\frac{J}{K\ kg} \text{ or } \frac{J}{K\ mole}$)
**Heat Capacity**: Temperature response per amount of heat added or subtracted ($\frac{J}{K}\text{ or } \frac{cal}{K}$) (cal = 4.19J)

##### Example
A 75g chunk of copper at 312C into 220g water at 12C. 

If the heat capacity of the beaker is 45cal/K and the, water, and copper are an isolated system, what is the final temperature.

$$Q_{water} + Q_{beaker} + Q_{copper} = 0$$
$Q_w = c_w m_w(T_f - T_{wi})$
$Q_b = C_b(T_f - T_{wi})$
$Q_c = c_cm_c(T_f-T_{ci})$
Where $c$ is specific heat, and $C$ is heat capacity.

$$T_f =\frac{c_cm_cT_{ci} + C_bT_{wi} + c_wm_wT_{wi}}{c_cm_c + C_b+c_wm_w}=19.6C$$
#### Heat Transfer Mechanisms 
Radiation is responsible for most of the heat transferred into a room from a fireplace.

Conduction is much slower, and convection also occurs through air flow due to temperature radiation.

#### Total Energy Under a Blackbody Curve
$$P = \frac{Q_{net}}{t} = \sigma eAT^4$$
$$P_{net} = \sigma eA(T_2^4 - T_1^4)$$
$$\sigma = 5.67\times 10^{-8}\frac{W}{m^2 T^4}$$
$$e = emissivity\text{ from 0 to 1}$$
$$A = \text{surface area}$$

#### Ideal Gas Law
Per Molecule:
$$PV = NkT$$
Per Mole:
$$PV = nRT$$
Work from a gas comes from changing the temperature, pressure, and/or volume.
$$T = \frac{PV}{nR}$$

#### Kinetic Theory of Gasses
For $N$ molecules of an ideal gas enclosed in a volume $V$, the product $PV$ is equal to 2 times the average kinetic energy of the molecules times the number of molecules. 

$$K = \left(\frac{1}{2}mv^2\right) = \frac{3}{2}kT$$
##### Maxwell Speed Distribution for Ideal Gas
$$f(v) = 4\pi\left[\frac{m}{2\pi RT}\right]^\frac{3}{2}v^2e^\frac{-mv^2}{2RT}$$
$$v_p = \sqrt{\frac{2RT}{M}}$$
$$\bar{v} = \sqrt{\frac{8RT}{\pi M}}$$
$$v_{rms} = \sqrt{\frac{3RT}{M}}$$

#### Heat and Work
$$W = \int PdV = \int_{V_{internal}}^{V_{final}}PdV$$
$$W = P\Delta V$$
#### First Law of Thermodynamics
$$\Delta E_{\text{internal}} = Q_{\text{in}} - W_{\text{out}}$$
$+Q$ = heat into system
$-Q$ = heat removed from the system
$+W$ = work by the system on environment
$-W$ work on the system by the environment

$$\Delta E_{\text{internal}} = R_{\text{internal,  final}} - E_{\text{internal,\ initial}} = Q-W$$
