#### Thermodynamics
If block A is at equilibrium and is in contact with block B and C, all blocks must be in in equilibrium.

The thermal energy of an object is associated with random motions of its molecules.

$$\text{heat}\equiv Q$$
$$Q =\Delta E_{internal}$$
$$Q = mc\Delta T$$
$$1 mol = 6\times 10^{23}\text{ things}$$
$$\text{calorie} = 4.184J$$
$$\text{kilocalorie} = 1 \text{ "food calorie"}$$
##### Heat Units
$$\text{Standard Chemistry:} \frac{cal}{g\ K}$$
$$\text{Standard Physics:} \frac{J}{kg\ K}$$
$$\text{Molar Specific:}\frac{J}{mol\ K} $$
##### Example
You want to cool 200 grams of water that is 25C, so you take 40g of ice and add it to the water. The ice is at -20C. What is the final temperature of the water?

Cooling: 200g water goes from 25C to $T_{final}$

Warming: 40g ice goes from -20C to 0C, then melts, then warms to $T_{final}$

$$(25C -T_f)m_wc_w = (0C-(-20C))m_ic_i + m_iL_f + m_ic_w(T_f-0C)$$
$$(25C)m_wc_w - 20Cm_ic_i - m_iL_f = T_fc_w(m_w + m_i)$$
$$T_f = \frac{(25C)m_wc_w - 20Cm_ic_i-m_iL_f}{c_w(m_w+m_i)}$$
$$T_f = \frac{\frac{(25C)200g\times 4.18J}{g\ K}-20C(40g)\left(\frac{2.05J}{g\ K}\right) - 40g\left(\frac{333.5J}{g}\right)}{\frac{4.18J}{g\ K}(200g + 40g)}$$
$$T_f = \frac{20,900J-640J-13,340J}{\frac{1003J}{K}} = 5.9C$$
