#### Intensity vs. Sound Level ($dB$)
$I = Intensity$

$$\beta\ \text{(Sound intesity \textbf{level})} = (10dB)\log_{10}\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right)$$
Where $I_0$ is a reference intensity with the value $1 \times 10^{-12} \frac{W}{m^2}$

#### Doppler Effect
When a frequency source is moving, the perceived frequency depends on the relative location.

$$f_{receiver} = \frac{v\pm u_{receiver}}{v\pm u_{source}}f_{source}$$
$v =$ speed of sound through air
$u_{receiver} =$ detector's speed relative to air
$u_{source} =$ source's speed relative to air 

##### Example
A stationary source $S$ generates circular outgoing waves on a lake. The wave speed is $5.0m/s$ and the crest-crest distance is $2.0m$. 

A person in a boat heads directly towards $S$ at $3.0m/s$. To this person, the frequency of the waves is:

$v_{source} = 5m/s$
$\lambda = 2m$
$v = \lambda f$
$f_{source} = \frac{5m/s}{2m} = 2.5Hz$
$f_{receiver} = \frac{5 + 3}{5 + 0}2.5s^{-1} = 4s^{-1} = 4Hz$
