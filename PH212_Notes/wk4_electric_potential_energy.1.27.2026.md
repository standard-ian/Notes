## Class 1
#### Potential Energy Continued
A positive charge in an electric field will experience a force in the direction of the field.

The electric force does positive work ($W$)

$$W_E = \vec{F}_E \cdot \Delta \vec{y} = F_E \Delta y cos\theta = F_E\Delta y = qE\Delta y$$
Where $\Delta y$ is just the change in distance the charge moves.

The Coulomb force is conservative, so the work done by it changes the **electric potential energy** by:
$$\Delta U = -W_E = -qE\Delta y$$
